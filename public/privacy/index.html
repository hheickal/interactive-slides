Privacy notice

For students taking part in interactive lecture slides.

These slides let you answer questions during a lecture from your own device. To do that, they record who answered what. This page explains exactly what is kept and who can see it.

What is collected

DataWhy
Your email address and name, from your Google accountTo check you are on the course roster and to attribute your answers
Your answers, with the question and a timestampLive results during the lecture, and participation or grades afterwards
Whether each answer was correctGrading and your own progress

Nothing else is requested from your Google account. No password is ever seen or stored — Google handles sign-in and only confirms your identity.

Polls marked “anonymous” are stored with no link to you at all. The system records that you voted, so you cannot vote twice, but not what you chose — and those two facts are kept apart in a way that cannot be rejoined, including by your instructor. Use those polls to answer honestly.

Who can see it

  • You — your own answers and your own score.
  • Your instructor — all answers for their own course.
  • Nobody — your identity on an anonymous poll.

Other students cannot see your answers. Access is enforced by the database itself, not by the website.

Where it is stored

In a Supabase project controlled by your instructor. It is not sold, not shared with advertisers, and not used for anything beyond the course.

How long it is kept

Your instructor sets a retention period for the course and deletes the data at the end of it. Ask them if you need the specifics for your course.

Your choices

  • Ask your instructor for a copy of what is held about you.
  • Ask for it to be deleted. Anonymous answers cannot be deleted individually, because there is no record of which ones are yours.
  • Not take part. Ask your instructor how participation is handled if you would rather not sign in.

Questions

Contact your course instructor, or your institution's data protection officer.

This page describes a teaching tool run by an individual instructor, not a commercial service.
